Project Overview

We are an interdisciplinary research group at VinUniversity, Vietnam, across the College of Engineering and Computer Science and the Center for Materials Innovation & Technology (CMIT). Our project builds VINMATICS-SC, a simulation-coupled vertical AI platform for the intelligent discovery and design of supercapacitor electrode materials and electrolyte systems.

Commercial supercapacitors deliver high power density (5–10 kW kg−1) and tens of thousands of charge–discharge cycles, yet their energy density (5–10 Wh kg−1) remains far lower than that of lithium-ion batteries. Closing this energy-density gap without sacrificing rate capability, cycle life, or intrinsic safety requires navigating a vast combinatorial space across porous and heteroatom-doped carbons, MXenes and 2D materials, transition-metal oxides/sulfides/hydroxides, conductive polymers, and metal-/covalent-organic frameworks (MOFs/COFs) that cannot be searched by trial-and-error synthesis.

VINMATICS-SC addresses this challenge through a closed-loop workflow coupling: (i) a normalized supercapacitor materials knowledge base, (ii) a high-throughput first-principles DFT and molecular dynamics engine (VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO, LAMMPS) accelerated by fine-tuned machine-learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs), (iii) uncertainty-calibrated AutoML property predictors, and (iv) a multi-objective, safety-constrained Bayesian experiment planner validated against real electrode synthesis and two-electrode device testing at VinUniversity.

Research Framework

Figure 1: VINMATICS-SC closed-loop discovery architecture coupling a curated supercapacitor data lake (WP1), high-throughput DFT/MD simulation and fine-tuned MLIPs (WP2), uncertainty-calibrated AutoML property prediction (WP3), and safety-constrained multi-objective active learning (WP4) validated in VinUniversity's electrochemistry laboratory (WP5).

WP1 · Data Foundation

Data Ingestion & Harmonisation

Aggregates literature, patents, open thermodynamic databases (Materials Project, AFLOW, OQMD, NOMAD), and internal CMIT experimental records into a context-aware ontology capturing electrode formulation, electrolyte, and cell configuration.

WP2 · Physics Simulation

High-Throughput DFT, MD & MLIPs

Automates prototype-aware structure generation (including SQS for disordered solid solutions), VASP/Quantum ESPRESSO electronic structure, constant-potential LAMMPS interface MD, and fine-tuned universal MLIPs (MACE, CHGNet, MatterSim).

WP3 · Surrogate Modeling

Uncertainty-Calibrated AutoML

Trains gradient-boosted trees, crystal graph neural networks (GNNs), and multi-task neural networks on material-family-grouped splits with calibrated confidence intervals and SHAP physical interpretability.

WP4 · Experiment Planning

Safety-Constrained Bayesian Planner

Performs multi-objective Bayesian optimization (qNEHVI) to maximize energy density and power density along the Pareto front while enforcing hard feasibility constraints on cycle-life retention, thermodynamic stability, and aqueous safety.

WP5 · Experimental Loop

Wet-Lab Synthesis & Device Testing

Synthesizes AI-recommended electrodes, characterizes phase and surface chemistry (XRD, SEM/EDS, XPS), and validates symmetric/asymmetric two-electrode devices across energy density, power density, 10,000-cycle retention, and safety.
